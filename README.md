# Ministry Outreach Network (Contract)

## Responsibilities

### Task Manager (Bryson DeLozier)
As our group’s Task Manager, I will ensure that our group makes progress at the appropriate pace, and that work is allocated evenly. I will make sure that everyone has a significant job, that everyone is able to complete their job, and that anyone needing assistance gets the help they need. I will ultimately ensure that our group functions in the proper manner as to ensure the Outreach Ministry Network’s satisfaction with our final product.

### Client Interfacer (Jordan Buchanan)
As the client interfacer, I will ensure that our group has adequate, professional, and open lines of communication with the client. I will be responsible for communicating the team's progress, asking questions about the client's expectations, and addressing concerns that they may have throughout development. I will also ensure that any feedback received from the client is shared with the group so that we can make necessary changes. Ultimately, my goal is to ensure that our final product meets the client's needs and expectations.

### Unit Tester (Luke Speer)
As a Unit Tester, I will encourage and invite the team to contribute to the project with unit tests, and ensure that the project is working behind the scenes. Unit Tests are essential to allow a bug-free application, ensuring that the end user can experience the application as intended. Also, writing unit tests gives time for reflection over the design choice, and gives time to rethink how the code being unit tested is written.

### Integration Tester (Bryson DeLozier, Luke Speer)
As a team, we will handle Integration Tests and to manage physical tests and test the app in daily life. This extends beyond Unit Tests, and goes into the realm of testing the app out by hand, and recording feedback of what can be improved or changed. Another responsibility will be to manage these tests where it is not biased or one person specific, and even potentially get small outside feedback from friends. Mainly, Integration Tests will focus on the look and feel of the app, rather than the code behind the scenes.

### Beta Test Organization (Bryson DeLozier, Jordan Buchanan, Luke Speer)
As a group, we will organize beta testing to ensure that our application works as intended and meets the client's expectations. We will gather feedback from testers, identify any bugs or issues, and determine what improvements need to be made before the final product is delivered. We will use this feedback to improve the functionality and user experience of our application.
